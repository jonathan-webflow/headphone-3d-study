HEADPHONE
(1) PRO

A teardown study, modelled from scratch in Blender. Scroll and it comes apart, one layer at a time.

Every control,
where your hand
expects it.

A roller, a paddle and a button. Learn them once, then use them blind.

9HGLASS HARDNESS

It starts
with glass.

A 9H window keeps the inside on show, and lifts away first.

A frame cut
from metal.

The aluminium bezel holds the glass and the arm in a single line.

The layer you
are meant to see.

Graphics printed on the inner plate, sitting right behind the glass.

Quiet work.

One board for Bluetooth, noise cancelling and power.

40MM DYNAMIC DRIVER

The part
that makes
the sound.

A dynamic driver with a PET dome, at the centre of everything.

1060MAH BATTERY

Tucked
under the driver.

A 1060 mAh lithium-ion cell, shaped to fit the cup.

Now from
the other side.

A baffle, a chassis and an aluminium tray close the acoustic chamber.

Soft where
it touches you.

A foam core wrapped in synthetic leather.

Eleven layers. One sound.

End of
teardown.

Eleven layers, back in one piece.
